Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: September 2026
This is a plain-language summary written for an early-stage product, not a substitute for legal advice. It covers what we collect through this site and the Pioneer Program application form today. A fuller Terms of Service will follow as AviaPulse adds paid self-serve signup and Stripe billing.

Who this covers

This policy applies to aviapulse.co and the AviaPulse application at aviapulse.vercel.app. AviaPulse is operated by Jason Verkennes, based in Murfreesboro, TN. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to jason@aviapulse.co.

What we collect

When you submit the form on this site (Pioneer Program application, demo request, or general info request), we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • What you're interested in (Pioneer Program, demo, or general info)
  • Any optional note you choose to add

If you become a customer and use the AviaPulse application itself, we additionally store the account, aircraft, maintenance, and compliance data you enter in order to provide the service — that's the core purpose of the product. We do not sell this data.

How we use it

  • To respond to your application or demo request personally.
  • To run the AviaPulse application for paying and Pioneer customers (tracking compliance items, sending alerts, generating logbook entries).
  • To show a public, anonymous count of how many people have applied to the Pioneer Program (no names or emails are ever shown publicly).

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers that run the service itself (currently Google Firebase/Cloud for hosting and data storage).

Data storage & security

Data is stored in Google Cloud's Firestore database and encrypted in transit and at rest through Google Cloud's infrastructure. Each customer's account data is isolated from every other customer's at the database level. AviaPulse has not yet pursued formal security certifications (such as SOC 2) — that's planned as the company grows, but the underlying access controls are built to keep customer data separated today.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your lead/application information at any time by emailing jason@aviapulse.co. If you're a customer, you can ask us to export or delete your account data the same way.

Pioneer Program terms (summary)

Pioneer Program participants receive free tracking for one aircraft, including the AD/SB Monitor add-on, while actively participating in the program. In exchange, participants agree to actually use the product, provide honest feedback to help us improve it, provide references to other potential customers, and provide a review of their experience — along with permission for AviaPulse to feature that review, their name, and their story (including, where applicable, what platform or process they switched from) in future marketing materials. This permission is requested explicitly and separately from the review itself, and participants may decline the marketing-use permission while still completing the rest of the program. Specific program terms (including how long the free period lasts) are communicated directly to each Pioneer applicant and may be adjusted as the program develops; the Pioneer Program has a limited number of spots and is accepted at AviaPulse's discretion.

Not a substitute for your mechanic or IA

AviaPulse is a compliance-tracking aid. It helps you stay organized and alerts you to what's coming due, but it does not replace the judgment, inspection, or sign-off of a licensed A&P mechanic or IA. Always verify compliance status against your own maintenance records before making airworthiness decisions.

Changes to this policy

As AviaPulse adds features — self-serve signup, billing, new integrations — this policy will be updated, and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
