What's actually built in

Compliance depth without the enterprise price tag.

Every item below is live in the product today — not a roadmap slide.

Auto-populated AD list from FAA manufacturing data

Upload the Type Certificate Data Sheet once for an airframe or engine model, and AviaPulse pulls the eligible engines, propellers, and appliances automatically — no competitor at any price point does this.

Daily automated AD scanning

Checks the FAA Federal Register every day — matching the Register's own real publishing pace — and flags genuinely new ADs before they become a surprise.

Digital logbook search

Upload scanned or handwritten logbook pages — AI reads them, flags anything uncertain for your review, and makes every page keyword-searchable in seconds.

Write-up to logbook entry, automatically

Report a squawk, resolve it, and AviaPulse generates a real FAA Part 43-compliant logbook entry — ready to sign.

Team accounts with real access control

Give mechanics their own logins with the right level of access — and revoke it instantly if someone leaves. No more sharing one password across a shop.

Before you ask

The questions every operator asks first.

How do I know my data is secure?

Your data is completely isolated from every other customer's at the database level — not just hidden in the interface. Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest through Google Cloud's infrastructure. We haven't gone through formal certifications like SOC 2 yet — that's something we'll pursue at a larger scale — but the fundamentals are built the right way from day one.

What actually separates this from CAMP or Veryon, besides price?

Those platforms are built for airlines and large fleets with dedicated maintenance departments — sales-gated pricing, and real users report onboarding takes 9+ weeks even for a single aircraft. AviaPulse is built around how a small operator actually works, and it uses AI to do the setup work those platforms still make you do by hand: it reads and digitizes your existing paper logbooks automatically, resolves the exact ADs eligible for your specific airframe and engine from FAA data instead of a generic checklist, and turns a resolved write-up into a ready-to-sign logbook entry. That's why onboarding here is a single sitting, not nine-plus weeks — and why the price is just the price on this page, not a number you get after a sales call.

Does this replace my mechanic or IA?

No — and it shouldn't. AviaPulse is a compliance-tracking aid that tells you what's coming due and helps you stay organized. Every inspection, sign-off, and airworthiness decision still rests with a licensed A&P or IA.

I'm not very technical — is this hard to set up?

No. Every account is set up personally — you're not filling out a signup form and figuring it out alone. Onboarding your first aircraft takes about 15 minutes, with someone walking you through it.
